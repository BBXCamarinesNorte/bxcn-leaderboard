⚖️ BXCN TOP 10 RANKING RULES

1. Primary ranking: Higher category finish count ranks higher.
2. Efficiency tie-breaker: If category finish counts are tied, the player with fewer Total Games ranks higher.
3. Secondary tie-breaker: If both category finishes and Total Games are tied, higher Total Finish Instances ranks higher.
4. Final tie-breaker: If still tied, players are listed alphabetically by IGN.

Example: Player 1 — 100 Games / 10 Spin vs Player 2 — 99 Games / 10 Spin. Player 2 ranks higher because they achieved the same Spin Finish count in fewer games.
